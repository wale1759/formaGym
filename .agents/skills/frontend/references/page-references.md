# Real-Page Reference Library

These links point to public product pages and design guidance. Each note identifies a trait worth studying; it does not certify the whole site as accessible, effective, or suitable for another product. Pages change, so check a live page before making claims about its current behavior. Use a supplied Figma design or user reference when it is more relevant.

Do not copy page layouts, wording, logos, images, illustrations, or code. Identify one or two transferable ideas, then adapt them to the user's audience, content, brand, and stack. Link the exact reference when mentioning it.

## Design discovery galleries

These galleries help find a small number of relevant live examples. A gallery listing is a discovery source, not proof that an example is usable, accessible, fast, or suitable for the project. Open the selected page and inspect it when the host provides visual browser access; see [the live reference inspection guide](live-reference-inspection.md).

### Awwwards

- **URL:** <https://www.awwwards.com/websites/>
- **Useful to study:** award-winning sites and browsable categories, tags, and technology labels. The directory includes Framer, GSAP, Three.js, WebGL, and 3D-related browsing options.
- **Adapt carefully:** awards emphasize craft and originality; check whether a page's motion and visual complexity fit this product, its users, and their devices.
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26.

### Framer Gallery

- **URL:** <https://www.framer.com/community/gallery/>
- **Useful to study:** live sites built with Framer, browsable by areas such as business, software, ecommerce, portfolio, organizations, places, and health.
- **Adapt carefully:** the gallery is useful for visual examples, but its pages are not automatically appropriate templates for a custom-coded project.
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26.

### Made With GSAP Showcase

- **URL:** <https://madewithgsap.com/showcase>
- **Useful to study:** live projects built with GSAP effects, including examples marked with website awards.
- **Adapt carefully:** inspect whether motion communicates or supports the content. Keep essential information usable without animation and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26.

### Mesh3d Gallery

- **URL:** <https://mesh3d.gallery/>
- **Useful to study:** curated interactive Three.js and real-time 3D website experiences, with links to the featured projects.
- **Adapt carefully:** 3D effects can add loading, device, and accessibility costs. Check the experience on a smaller screen and keep a clear path to the page's main task.
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26.

### Three.js Examples

- **URL:** <https://threejs.org/examples/>
- **Useful to study:** official Three.js implementation demos and visual techniques.
- **Adapt carefully:** these are technical examples, not a gallery of complete product websites. Use them to understand an effect, then make sure it serves the project's content and task.
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26.

## Learning

### Duolingo home

- **URL:** <https://www.duolingo.com/>
- **Useful to study:** clear learning promise, short descriptions of progress and feedback, playful tone, and motivation cues.
- **Adapt carefully:** a professional course or classroom product may need a calmer visual voice; do not copy its mascot, brand colors, or reward mechanics.
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26.
### Khan Academy home

- **URL:** <https://www.khanacademy.org/>
- **Useful to study:** clear audience paths for students, families, and teachers; visible subject discovery; and explanation of how learning works.
- **Adapt carefully:** choose only the audience paths needed by the project; keep the first screen focused.
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26.

## Finance and pricing

### Stripe pricing

- **URL:** <https://stripe.com/pricing>
- **Useful to study:** pricing tiers and transaction costs organized for comparison, with feature and fee details near the relevant offer.
- **Adapt carefully:** pricing and payment terms must come from the actual project; never reuse Stripe's prices, claims, or brand treatment.
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26.

## Travel and booking

### Airbnb search results

- **URL:** <https://www.airbnb.com/s/homes>
- **Useful to study:** search, filters, listing comparison, image-led results, and the way price, dates, location, and ratings support a booking decision.

- **Checked:** 2026-10-26.

## Developer tools and productivity

### Linear features

- **URL:** <https://linear.app/features>
- **Useful to study:** grouping a broad product into understandable capability areas, concise feature explanations, and repeated next steps.
- **Adapt carefully:** keep the number of sections proportional to the product; use real screenshots or specific examples instead of decorative interface mockups.
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26.

### GitHub Issues feature page

- **URL:** <https://github.com/features/issues>
- **Useful to study:** explaining project views such as boards, tables, roadmaps, and keyboard interactions in terms of the work users do.
- **Adapt carefully:** avoid adding views or shortcuts without a matching user need; check real interaction states in the target project.
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26.

## Commerce

### Shopify Editions, Spring 2026

- **URL:** <https://www.shopify.com/editions/spring2026>

## Forms and public-service flows

### GOV.UK Design System patterns

- **URL:** <https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/>
- **Useful to study:** task-focused patterns for asking for information, account creation, validation recovery, confirmation, and service navigation. The patterns include written guidance and coded examples.
- **Adapt carefully:** start from the user's actual service and legal requirements; government content and styles may not suit a commercial product.
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26.
